Is ADWIN actually better? (paired Wilcoxon, α=0.05)

ADWIN's edge over EDDM is a cost story (accuracy never significantly different); its edge over DDM/KSWIN is an accuracy story — and not always free. With only 10 seeds, "not significant" means "not distinguishable at this sample size," not proven equivalence.

Ablation: is detection worth it?

No retraining, vs. a naive fixed schedule every 5,000 samples, vs. the proposed ADWIN-triggered system. 10 seeds per condition.

On both abrupt-drift streams, naive periodic retraining is statistically competitive with detection — sometimes numerically ahead, at a lower and perfectly predictable cost. Detection's advantage is concentrated in gradual drift.
